How to Use This Simulator

Goal: Explore how Sample Size ($N$) influences P-values and Clinical Significance.

  • 1. Adjust Parameters: Change the Mean walking speeds and Standard Deviations for two groups of prosthetic users.
  • 2. Titrate Sample Size: Move the slider to see how increasing $N$ makes even tiny differences "Statistically Significant" ($p < 0.05$).
  • 3. Watch the Charts:
    • Left Chart: Shows the population overlap. High overlap = Low clinical impact.
    • Right Chart: Shows the Confidence Interval. If it crosses 0, the result is not significant.
  • 4. Evaluate Effect Size: Watch the orange "Effect Size" card to see if the difference is actually meaningful in a clinic.

Origin: Developed by Prof. Fedel using Gemini 3 Flash (2026) for Prosthetics & Orthotics education.
